Citing LION
===========

If LION helps your research, please cite the repository (a paper is to come):

.. code-block:: bibtex

   @software{LION,
     title  = {{LION}: Learned Iterative Optimization Networks},
     author = {Biguri, Ander and Shoebridge, Charlie and Runkel, Christina and Vu, Thanh Trung and Valat, Emilien and Sherry, Ferdia and Tan, Hong Ye and Sainz Bear, Andrea and Kiss, Maximilian B.},
     url    = {https://github.com/CambridgeCIA/LION},
   }

Each method in LION also cites its paper: ``model.cite()`` gives its BibTeX
(``model.cite("MLA")`` the MLA form), and a published package lists every
citation of what it used.
