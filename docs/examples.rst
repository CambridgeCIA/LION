Example scripts
===============

Short, complete programs, one feature of LION each, in
`scripts/example_scripts <https://github.com/CambridgeCIA/LION/tree/main/scripts/example_scripts>`_:
supervised training, an unrolled method, self-supervised training, a
Plug-and-Play denoiser, evaluating and comparing methods, resuming and
fine-tuning, publishing and reproducing, and your own pieces. They train on
LIDC-IDRI and need a GPU; their README says how to run them.
