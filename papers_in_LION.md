# Papers in LION

The methods LION implements, by kind, with their papers, are on the
documentation site: [Methods in LION](https://cambridgecia.github.io/LION/methods.html)
(source: [`docs/methods.rst`](docs/methods.rst)).

Every method from a paper also cites it in code: `Class.cite()` gives its
BibTeX, `Class.cite("MLA")` the MLA form.
